Spec highlights layered on top (`2026-08-28-web-uiux-design.md:15-22`, HANDOFF.md:47-48): no em dashes anywhere (copy, comments, docs); no lucide icons; no glassmorphism-by-default; no fake testimonials or invented numbers; no sparkle icons or decorative arrows; no hover-everything; required: real product screenshots, skeleton loaders, Terms and Privacy pages, honest empty/loading/error states that name the cause and the next action.

Conflicts the owner already ruled on (memory note, DESIGN.md "Design language"): the plum background is kept deliberately over the purple-and-black caution; glass is capped at two accents rather than banned; the radius tiers are the answer to "soft-corner-radius-everywhere".

Status against this list after the audit: items 8 (finding 1, 2, 3), 10 (finding 41), 15 and 22 (findings 30, 31, glyphs and arrows), and 19 (finding 46, the gradient hero stand-ins) have live hits. Everything else on the list is clean.

## D. Design constraints SP7 (fan discovery) must carry

These are the locks and dials the fan home, feed, and search surfaces inherit. Each is traceable to DESIGN.md, the 9A/9B rulings, or a finding above.

**Dials and anatomy locks**
- ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1 (DESIGN.md Design Read). A fan feed is the most tempting place to drift to ENERGY 3 (auto-playing media, parallax posters, infinite carousels). It may not. The only carousel in the product is the landing hero, and it exists for signed-out marketing photography.
- One focal point per screen. The fan home's single ember element is the action for tonight (open or buy the show); everything else defers. Today's `(fan)/index.tsx` has zero accents and `TicketList` can show several (finding 43): SP7 sets it to exactly one.
- Card anatomy is owner-locked (DESIGN.md Core components, sp9a rulings). A show or event card on a fan surface reuses `GigCard`'s skeleton: photo top with `--gk-scrim`, a real-state badge on the photo (`Badge variant="accent"` only over the scrim), Syne title, muted venue/neighborhood line, at most two chips, date muted left, price as the filled ember pill right ("From $12" / "Free"). Never a price on a `MusicianCard`. No lift, no zoom, no shadow on hover; border warms and scrim lightens only. Mobile ports the same anatomy over `PhotoPlaceholder` until a photo is wired (9B ruling 2).
- Schedule lists use `DateBlockRow` (46px date chip, ember month via `--gk-focus`, Syne day numeral); the artist and venue "Upcoming events" rows already do (`MusicianProfile.tsx:65-74`, `CuratorProfile.tsx:181-190`). A fan "this week" list is a schedule list.
- Radius tiers: 999px only for the primary CTA and chips, 10px cards and inputs, 6px badges and small controls. No 8px or 12px (finding 4).
- Status tints stay at three plus neutral; the event/ticket tone maps in `eventDisplay.ts` on both platforms are the vocabulary. No "featured" or "hot" color. Badges carry real state only (R-09): "Tonight", "Sold out", "Free" are real; "Popular" is not unless it is computed from real data and the computation is named.

**Effect caps**
- Glass: zero new uses. Both product-wide slots are spent (landing nav, mini-player); a sticky filter bar, a floating "tonight" pill, or a bottom sheet on the fan home must be solid `--gk-surface` (the SP6 sticky buy bar is the precedent, `BuyTicketsFlow.tsx:109-113`).
- Shadows only on overlays (sheet, dialog, popover). A fan card grid is flat.
- No section restacks the page gradient; the scrim is the only gradient a fan card gets.
- Photo-forward surfaces keep the always-dark scrim in both themes; on-scrim text uses `tokens.dark.text` (9B ruling 2).

**Accent dosage**
- Ember only on: the primary CTA, a price pill, the active tab, a status-badge tint over the scrim. Never on borders, body links, or decorative icons (findings 41, 42), never as bare text on a light surface (finding 17): use `--gk-focus` / `t.focus` in light or the filled chip.
- Selection states (a chosen filter chip, a chosen date) use the filled chip, not an ember outline.

**Copy and honesty**
- No em dashes; en dashes for numeric ranges and middots as separators are the sanctioned glyphs.
- No "Discover", "Explore", "Get started" CTAs (finding 38). Specific verbs: "Find shows near you", "See who's playing Friday", "Get tickets".
- Empty states name the cause and one action in the night-life voice ("Nothing on the books tonight. Try Friday, or widen the radius."). Loading is a content-shaped skeleton. Errors say what failed and offer Retry (findings 10 to 16 are the shape to avoid).
- No invented counts ("1,200 shows this week"), no "trending" without a real signal, no fake social proof, no city name until the launch metro is real (`LandingSections.tsx:188-193`).
- Money sentences on fan surfaces reuse `formatTierPrice`, `tierFeeLine`, and `eventSalesClosedReason` verbatim on both platforms; byte parity is the contract (SP5 ruling, 9A ruling 8, 9B ruling 7).
- Sentence case everywhere, including tab titles (finding 34).

**Accessibility floor**
- 44px targets on every Pressable and chip (findings 19, 20); `accessibilityRole` and a label on every mobile Pressable (finding 23); `id`/`htmlFor` on every web filter input (finding 22); Enter submits a search form (finding 21); reduced motion honored on any sheet the search surface opens (finding 25); status never conveyed by color or opacity alone (finding 26).
- Both themes verified, with the light-theme ember rules above.

**What a fan home, feed, or search surface may and may not do**
- May: a photo-forward grid or list of tonight's and this week's shows; a date strip built from `DateBlockRow`; filter chips (genre, day, neighborhood) as `Chip`; a map only if sub-8's geo work lands first; the ticket-holder "Your upcoming shows" list already on Home (`(fan)/index.tsx:84-95`), which is real data and stays.
- May not: a bento mosaic, a stats row, a "trusted by" strip, a hero carousel, auto-playing audio or video in the feed, glass anywhere, an ember-bordered "featured" card, a recommendation label with no real signal behind it, a "Messages" or "Search" tab that still says coming soon after SP7 ships (finding 8: replace the placeholders, do not add to them).
- Web has no fan routes today (9A ruling 9 reconciled spec 6.11 as empty); `/gigs` is the public browse and `/e/[eventId]` is the public event page. If SP7 adds a web fan surface it takes the signed-in shell (`SHELL_PREFIXES`), the same `GigCard`, and the sticky solid bar; if it does not, the sp7 rulings should say so explicitly.
